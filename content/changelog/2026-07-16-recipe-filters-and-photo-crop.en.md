## Smarter Recipe Filters

The camera filter on the recipes page is now sorted by sensor generation (newest first). Picking a sensor generation also narrows the camera list to match, so you only see cameras that actually belong to it.

## More Reliable Camera & Film Simulation Info

Uploading a recipe with a brand-new camera or a less common film simulation no longer risks losing that info — it's now saved automatically instead of silently dropping from your recipe.

## Square Photo Previews

Recipe photo carousels now display as clean 1:1 square crops for a more consistent look across the gallery.
